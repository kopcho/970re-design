# grok-01 — Listing Detail (refined)

## What changed from the first pass
You showed the earlier light/teal mocks and said you liked the direction. This version keeps that clean, modern, high-trust feel and pushes it further while locking in the product truths.

## Type
- **Price & quotes:** Iowan Old Style → Palatino → Georgia (serif). The agent’s words need presence.
- **UI:** system sans for everything else. Clean and fast.
- **Labels / precision:** ui-monospace for evidence tags and microcopy.

## Design decisions
1. **Exact Match is still the badge**, but the *quote* is the proof. No bare percentage. Every confirmed gem shows the agent’s own words.
2. Light surface + deep ink = the readable, premium feel from the mocks you liked, without looking like every other real-estate app.
3. Dual CTAs kept strong (“Schedule a look” + “Text Rich”) — lead-gen is woven in, not bolted on.
4. Re970 gets its own burnt-orange treatment so it feels like a signature action.
5. Neighborhood / map peek stays, but secondary to the evidence.
